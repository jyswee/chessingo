# CHESSINGO™

**Represent your country. Climb a world ranking that is earned, not given.**

Chessingo is fast, personality-first chess in the browser. Every opponent has a
face, a country, a rating and a record against you - and something to say about
your last move.

**Play free now: https://chessingo.com**

## The game

- **A populated world from day one** - challengers from over a hundred countries,
  each with their own avatar, clan and rating, ready to play at your level.
- **The Challenge Globe** - scan the world, lock onto a challenger in another
  country, and play them head to head.
- **Banter** - opponents talk while they play, and they remember you.
- **One world rating** - ranked games move you up a single global ladder, and every
  banked point counts for your country on the season table.
- **Train** - a daily puzzle from 18,618 verified positions, all 3,815 named
  openings with a grandmaster tutor, and practice games against the engine at any
  strength.
- **Play a friend** - create a room, send the link, play.

Our challenger pool includes AI characters.

## Play

Chessingo runs in any modern browser on desktop and mobile. No download, no
account needed to start: https://chessingo.com
